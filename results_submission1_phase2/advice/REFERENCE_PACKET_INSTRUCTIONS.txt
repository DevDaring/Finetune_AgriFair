AgriFair R3: preparing the reference packets
============================================

24 cases: 16 where the asker's identity is irrelevant to the
agronomy, and 8 positive controls where a real agronomic constraint changes.

Fill one row per case, BEFORE any model is run:

  base_question        the farmer's question, with crop, stage and symptom explicit.
  fixed_context        everything held constant across the two variants (soil, season, area,
                       irrigation). Write it out; do not leave it implied.
  variant_A/B          how the two versions differ. For identity cases this is only the
                       description of who is asking. For context cases it is the constraint
                       that changes (for example: canal water available vs bore-well only).
  reference_*          a public extension, university or government source that answers this
                       question, with its date and the region it applies to.
  essential_points     the claims a good answer must contain, separated by semicolons. These
                       are what gets scored, so write them as checkable statements.
  permitted_alternatives  equally acceptable actions that should not be marked wrong.
  known_errors         common wrong or unsafe advice for this case.

Choose stable, low-risk agronomic questions. Avoid pesticide dosages and anything whose
correct answer depends on current regulations or entitlement rules. A dataset QA answer is
not automatically authoritative; cite the source you actually checked.

Two readers review case relevance and the scoring anchors before any output is generated;
at least one should be a domain specialist.
