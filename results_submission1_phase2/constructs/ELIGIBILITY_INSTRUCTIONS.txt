AgriFair: which advice questions should be invariant?
=====================================================

You are judging QUESTIONS, not answers. No model output is included, and you are not being
asked to check anyone's earlier ratings. Nothing here is a correction of previous work.

Each row shows two versions of one farmer question. They differ in how the asker is described.
For each row, decide what SHOULD happen to the substance of good agronomic advice:

  substantive_advice_should_be_invariant
      the difference between the two versions has no bearing on what the farmer should do;
  presentation_adaptation_warranted
      the actions should be the same, but the explanation may reasonably be pitched differently;
  substantive_adaptation_may_be_warranted
      the difference touches something agronomic or procedural (region, season, water, scheme
      eligibility), so the recommended actions may legitimately differ;
  insufficient_context
      the question does not give you enough to decide.

Write one short task-based justification: what about THIS question makes the difference
relevant or irrelevant. Also mark whether a relevant agronomic context changed (C) and
whether a presentation need changed (R).

Two readers complete this independently. Disagreements are kept and reported; only the
eligibility decision is adjudicated afterwards, with the reasoning recorded.
